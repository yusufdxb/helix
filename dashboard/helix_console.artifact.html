What this is

Every event here was recorded on a live Unitree GO2 EDU at CaresLab on 23 April 2026, with the full four-tier stack running on the onboard Jetson. Nothing is simulated or re-enacted: 30 faults, 14 recovery hints, 14 recovery actions, 3,064 zero-twist commands.

How to read it

The detector watches publish rate, not content. When a metric drifts past z = 3.0 for three consecutive samples it emits an ANOMALY. Rule R1 turns LiDAR-family anomalies into STOP_AND_HOLD; R2 clears them on a quiet window. The envelope is the last gate before any command reaches the robot.

The 147-second hold

From t+161.7 to t+309.2 the robot is held still far longer than intended. Three RESUMEs were rejected as SUPPRESSED_COOLDOWN, because a RESUME was being checked against the cooldown of the very STOP it was trying to clear. This bag predates the fix. RESUME is now exempt from cooldown (commit 052bd24), with regression tests. The console shows the bug rather than hiding it.

Known limits

The robot stood idle for the whole run. All thirty anomalies are natural jitter, and they produced nine STOP_AND_HOLD events in seven minutes, which is too many. The threshold was raised to 4.0 and a min-duration gate added afterwards; it is still being tuned. The trace panel plots /sportmodestate rate, the one continuous series recovered from the bag; per-metric detector windows survive only at fault instants and are drawn as ticks. /helix/cmd_vel had no subscriber here, so "the robot holds" is proven end to end through the software path but not yet through twist_mux to the motors.
